## Dónde consultar

La documentación oficial, en inglés.

- **Varios tamaños de pantalla:** [las listas y cuadrículas (`LazyVerticalGrid`)](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/lists), [diseños adaptables](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/layouts/adaptive) y [qué son los dp](https://developer.android.com/training/multiscreen/screendensities).
- **Room:** [guardar datos con Room](https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/room), [KSP](https://developer.android.com/build/migrate-to-ksp) y [el Database Inspector](https://developer.android.com/studio/inspect/database).
- **Corrutinas y Flow:** [corrutinas en Android](https://developer.android.com/kotlin/coroutines), [Flow](https://developer.android.com/kotlin/flow) y [StateFlow y `stateIn`](https://developer.android.com/kotlin/flow/stateflow-and-sharedflow).
- **ViewModel y navegación:** [ViewModel](https://developer.android.com/topic/libraries/architecture/viewmodel) y [Navigation](https://developer.android.com/guide/navigation).
- **Permisos:** [pedir permisos mientras se usa la app](https://developer.android.com/training/permissions/requesting).
